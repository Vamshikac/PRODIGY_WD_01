Started in a garage, 2019

Kaffeine opened with one espresso machine and four tables, run by two people who quit their jobs over a bad cup of coffee at a work conference. Seven years later it's still four tables — we'd rather get the coffee right than get bigger.

We roast in small batches so nothing sits in a bag for months before it reaches your cup.
